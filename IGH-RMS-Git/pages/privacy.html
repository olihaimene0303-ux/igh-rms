Privacy Notice

How IGH-RMS intends to handle information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

If you contact IGH-RMS, we may receive information such as your name, organization, email address, telephone number and the details of your enquiry. We use this information to respond to the enquiry, assess potential assignments and communicate about requested services.

Website measurement

Our planned production analytics is Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first measurement service designed to report aggregate website performance and visitor metrics without collecting or using visitors' personal data. Analytics will be enabled only after the production domain and Cloudflare site are configured.

Third-party services

The production website may use third-party infrastructure for hosting, email, security and analytics. Configuration will be documented and reviewed before launch.

Data minimisation

IGH-RMS should collect only information reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry or operate the website. Do not submit confidential, classified, financial-account, password or other highly sensitive information through a general website enquiry.

Retention

Enquiry information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for business follow-up, contractual, accounting, compliance or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Updates

This notice will be reviewed when the website, enquiry workflow, analytics or applicable requirements materially change.
